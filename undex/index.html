Meet Rodica

AN OLYMPIAN
IN YOUR CORNER

🏅 Olympic High Diver — Romania

Rodica Matthews grew up in Romania where she trained as a competitive high diver, eventually representing her country at the Olympic level. The discipline, focus, and deep understanding of the human body that sport demands became the foundation for everything she does today.

After relocating to the United States, Rodica turned her athletic expertise into a calling. She founded SOMA — Science Of Movement Applied — as a way to bring real, research-backed training to everyday people in Southampton and the surrounding Hamptons community.

"I believe every single person has an athlete inside them. My job is to find that person and bring them out."

What sets Rodica apart is not just her Olympic background. It is her warmth. Her clients describe her as endlessly encouraging, genuinely invested, and the kind of trainer who remembers your name, your goals, and your story. She does not run cookie-cutter programs. She listens, she adapts, and she shows up for you every time.

She has worked with people in their teens and people in their seventies. She has helped new moms rebuild their strength, helped retirees rediscover mobility they thought was gone, and pushed competitive athletes past plateaus they could not crack on their own. No matter where you are starting from, Rodica has been there with someone just like you.

What We Offer

TRAINING FOR EVERY GOAL

SOMA is for everyone. Young, older, just starting out, or coming back after years away. Rodica has a program that fits your life and your body right now.

🏋️

Private Training

One on one sessions with Rodica built entirely around you. Your goals, your pace, your schedule. This is the most direct path to transformation because every minute of every session is focused on exactly what your body needs. Beginners are absolutely welcome. In fact, many of Rodica's most devoted clients started with zero fitness experience and no idea where to begin.

👥

Group Classes

There is something powerful about training with other people. Rodica's group classes are energetic, fun, and welcoming to all fitness levels. You will push harder than you would alone, laugh along the way, and leave feeling like part of a community. Classes are kept small so Rodica can give real attention to every participant in the room.

🎯

Weight Loss Programs

Real, lasting weight loss comes from understanding your body, not from punishing it. Rodica builds programs that combine movement, strength work, and smart habits to help you reach and stay at a healthy weight. No crash diets. No unrealistic expectations. Just steady, sustainable progress that you can actually maintain for the rest of your life.

🧘

Flexibility and Mobility

This is one of the most overlooked parts of fitness, and one of the most important. Whether you are dealing with stiffness, recovering from an injury, or simply want to move through your day with more ease and less pain, Rodica's mobility work draws on her Olympic training background to restore and improve how your body moves.

💪

Strength and Conditioning

Building strength is not just for young people or athletes. Strong muscles protect your joints, improve your posture, boost your metabolism, and make every part of daily life easier. Rodica designs strength programs that are appropriate for your current level and progress at a rate that challenges you without overwhelming you.

🥗

Nutrition Guidance

Exercise and nutrition go hand in hand. Rodica offers practical, personalized guidance on fueling your body to support your training, your energy levels, and your long term health. This is not about strict meal plans or eliminating everything you love. It is about understanding what your body needs and making choices that feel good and sustainable.

NOT SURE WHERE TO START?

That is completely okay. Rodica offers a free first consultation where you can come in, talk about your goals, ask every question you have, and get a feel for whether SOMA is right for you. There is no pressure and no commitment. Just an honest conversation about where you are and where you want to go.
